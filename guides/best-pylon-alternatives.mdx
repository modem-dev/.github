Pylon gets evaluated by B2B teams whose support lives in Slack Connect, and the alternatives search usually starts with one of three frictions: pricing isn't published anywhere (every plan is a sales conversation, with no self-serve trial listed), adopting it means replacing your support tooling wholesale, or what you actually wanted was the product signal inside support conversations, not a new helpdesk.

We build Modem, one of the five below. Modem competes with Pylon on the product-signal job, not the helpdesk job, and the ranking reflects our angle, so read accordingly. A section on where Pylon still wins is included.

## 1. Modem

[Modem](https://modem.dev/) is the alternative for teams that realize what they wanted from Pylon was the clustered product signal, requests and issues surfaced from support traffic with the accounts attached, without replacing the support tool to get it. Modem reads the same [shared Slack channels](https://modem.dev/integrations/slack), plus [Microsoft Teams](https://modem.dev/integrations/microsoft-teams), [Discord](https://modem.dev/integrations/discord), [Zendesk](https://modem.dev/integrations/zendesk), [Intercom](https://modem.dev/integrations/intercom), [email](https://modem.dev/integrations/email), and [calls](https://modem.dev/integrations/gong), clusters duplicates into ranked topics, and files consolidated [Linear or Jira issues](https://modem.dev/integrations/linear) with the customer quotes attached. When the fix merges, it [closes the loop](https://modem.dev/close-the-loop) back into the channels where the reports landed.

Pricing is public: flat usage tiers, unlimited seats, from $80/month plus a free tier, with a self-serve start instead of a demo.

**Where it fits:** teams keeping their current support setup who want the product-signal and engineering-handoff layer. **Where it doesn't:** Modem doesn't resolve support tickets, no deflection, no macros, no queue. If you need the helpdesk replaced, pick from the three below. Direct comparison: [Modem vs Pylon](https://modem.dev/pylon-alternative).

## 2. Plain

[Plain](https://www.plain.com/) is the closest platform-for-platform swap with a self-serve door: an API-first B2B support stack ("no-code or all-code") with the Ari agent for triage and resolution, Sidekick for assisting humans, and auto-surfaced insights. Foundation, at $35/seat/month, already includes Linear and Jira integrations and 2,000 monthly AI credits, with a 7-day trial and no credit card, the transparency Pylon doesn't offer.

The composable framing means more configuration up front, and Discord plus SSO/SCIM sit on the custom Frontier tier.

**Where it fits:** teams that want to shape their own support motion on modern infrastructure. See our [Pylon vs Plain vs Thena breakdown](https://modem.dev/guides/pylon-vs-plain-vs-thena).

## 3. Thena

[Thena](https://www.thena.ai/) is the predictable-pricing alternative: a conventional ticket queue unifying Slack, email, and chat, with AI as the baseline on every plan, detection, summaries, tagging, and an assistant included even on the $29/user/month Starter (up to 5 seats, 1,000 tickets/month). Standard at $79 adds AI web chat, an agent studio, and MCP access; Enterprise adds Teams support and custom agent deployments.

Clear ticket caps make cost easy to estimate, and they're also the constraint as volume grows.

**Where it fits:** teams that want Slack-native support with AI included and a bill they can predict.

## 4. Zendesk

[Zendesk](https://www.zendesk.com/) is the incumbent answer, and for ticket-volume operations it's still the deepest bench: [intelligent triage](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4964463770650-About-intelligent-triage) classifies incoming tickets by intent and sentiment, real-time dashboards have added AI anomaly detection, and the app ecosystem (Jira linking included) covers most workflows. Slack-based B2B support is possible but bolted on rather than native, which is usually why Pylon was on the table in the first place.

**Where it fits:** orgs at a scale where the ecosystem and ops tooling outweigh Slack-nativeness. Modem [pairs with Zendesk](https://modem.dev/integrations/zendesk) for the product-signal layer; see our [Zendesk mining guide](https://modem.dev/guides/best-tools-to-mine-feedback-from-zendesk-tickets).

## 5. Intercom

[Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/) is the messenger-first platform: chat-led support with AI resolution (Fin), and Jira/Linear apps that let agents create and link issues from a conversation. For B2B teams whose customers arrive through in-product chat rather than shared Slack channels, it's the natural fit, and the same caveat applies in reverse: Slack Connect support isn't its center.

**Where it fits:** chat-led support desks. Modem [ingests Intercom conversations](https://modem.dev/integrations/intercom) for the layer above; see our [Intercom mining guide](https://modem.dev/guides/best-tools-to-mine-feedback-from-intercom-conversations).

## Where Pylon still wins

For B2B support run natively in Slack Connect and Teams at breadth, seven channels including SMS, WhatsApp, and phone, with four scoped AI agents (Assist, Background, Slack, Support) investigating and acting, and workflow automation described in natural language, Pylon is the most complete agentic package of this set, and it claims 1,500+ B2B customers. The alternatives exist because of the sales-gated pricing and the size of the commitment, not because the product underdelivers on its category.

## How to choose

Separate the helpdesk decision from the intelligence decision. Replacing the helpdesk: Plain (composable, self-serve), Thena (predictable seats, AI included), Zendesk or Intercom (incumbent depth, chat-led respectively). Keeping the helpdesk and adding what Pylon's clustering promised, product signal, ARR context, engineering handoff: that's [Modem](https://modem.dev/pylon-alternative), layered over whichever support stack you already run. If you can't get a price without a call and that's a dealbreaker, note that Plain, Thena, and Modem all publish theirs.

## FAQ

### What's the cheapest Pylon alternative?

Among tools with published pricing: Thena from $29/user/month, Plain from $35/seat/month, and Modem's flat tiers from $80/month with unlimited seats (plus a free tier), cheapest depends on team size, since Modem doesn't bill per seat.

### Can I get Pylon-style feature-request clustering without switching helpdesks?

Yes, that's Modem's exact shape: it clusters requests and bugs across your existing support tool, Slack, and other channels into ranked topics with accounts attached, and files them to [Linear or Jira](https://modem.dev/integrations/linear). Your queue stays where it is.

### Which alternatives work in Slack Connect channels?

Plain and Thena both run support in Slack; Modem reads shared Slack channels as a signal source (without being the helpdesk). Zendesk and Intercom treat Slack as an add-on channel. Our [Pylon vs Plain vs Thena comparison](https://modem.dev/guides/pylon-vs-plain-vs-thena) covers the Slack-native trio in depth.
