Pendo alternatives searches are usually un-bundling searches. The suite covers product analytics, in-app guides, and feedback in one platform, which is exactly right for teams standardizing, and exactly wrong for teams that want one of the three without instrumenting their app for the whole. The alternatives below split by which job sent you looking.

We build Modem, one of the six, and it competes with Pendo's feedback module (not its analytics or guides), so weigh our ranking with that disclosed. Where Pendo still wins is covered at the end.

## The short version

| Tool | Which Pendo job it replaces | Feedback mechanism | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Modem | Feedback, aimed at engineering | Read from Slack, Discord, support, calls | Feedback that becomes tracker tickets |
| PostHog | Product analytics (+ surveys) | In-product surveys | Devtools teams, self-serve analytics |
| Sprig | Behavior-targeted research | Surveys triggered by behavior | Research on specific usage cohorts |
| Fullstory | Behavioral analytics + replay | Guides and surveys in-experience | Auto-detected friction |
| Contentsquare (Hotjar) | UX diagnosis | Widget feedback + surveys | Debugging specific flows |
| Canny | The feedback portal | Board posts and votes | Customer-visible request queue |

## 1. Modem

[Modem](https://modem.dev/) replaces the feedback half for teams whose feedback doesn't arrive through an in-app portal in the first place. It reads [Slack](https://modem.dev/integrations/slack), [Discord](https://modem.dev/integrations/discord), [support tickets](https://modem.dev/integrations/zendesk), [email](https://modem.dev/integrations/email), and [sales calls](https://modem.dev/integrations/gong), clusters duplicates into ranked topics with the customers attached, and files consolidated [Linear or Jira issues](https://modem.dev/integrations/linear), no instrumentation, since it connects to sources rather than your app's code. Through the [PostHog integration](https://modem.dev/integrations/posthog), the agent checks what customers said against what they actually did, the pairing Pendo does natively, assembled from the tools you already run.

Setup is the sharpest contrast: connect existing sources versus instrument your application. Pricing is flat usage tiers from $80/month plus a free tier.

**Where it fits:** engineering-led teams whose feedback lives in conversations, wanting fixes routed rather than dashboards. **Where it doesn't:** Modem has no analytics of its own and no in-app guides; it queries [PostHog](https://modem.dev/integrations/posthog) for the former and doesn't do the latter. Direct comparison: [Modem vs Pendo](https://modem.dev/pendo-alternative).

## 2. PostHog

[PostHog](https://posthog.com/) is the engineering-culture replacement for Pendo's analytics core: event analytics, session replay, feature flags, experiments, and [surveys](https://posthog.com/surveys) in one self-serve platform with usage-based pricing and a generous free tier. Survey responses connect natively to analytics and replay, so "who said this and what were they doing" is answerable inside one product.

There's no in-app guides/onboarding module, which is often the specific Pendo feature teams discover they can drop.

**Where it fits:** devtools and engineering-led teams that want analytics they can self-serve into. Modem [pairs with PostHog](https://modem.dev/guides/best-tools-to-pair-product-analytics-with-customer-feedback) for the conversational-feedback layer.

## 3. Sprig

[Sprig](https://sprig.com/) replaces the research slice: in-product surveys targeted by behavior and event history, a survey can trigger for users who abandoned a specific flow, with sessions recorded alongside responses and AI grouping the open-text themes.

It's a research tool, not a suite: no product-analytics platform underneath, no guides.

**Where it fits:** research-led teams that want to ask the right cohort at the right moment.

## 4. Fullstory

[Fullstory](https://www.fullstory.com/platform/) covers the behavioral side with a different emphasis: session replay across web and mobile with frustration and sentiment signals layered on top, so friction gets flagged even when nobody files a report, plus guides and surveys collected in-experience.

Feedback is the secondary layer here, the reverse of Pendo Feedback's portal-first shape.

**Where it fits:** teams that want friction detected automatically rather than reported.

## 5. Contentsquare (formerly Hotjar)

[Contentsquare](https://contentsquare.com/hotjar/), which absorbed Hotjar, pairs heatmaps and session recordings with a feedback widget and surveys, built for visual UX diagnosis: watch where someone hesitated, read what they said about it, in the same review.

It answers "what's wrong with this flow," not "which customers asked for what," and doesn't connect feedback to CRM or revenue data.

**Where it fits:** teams debugging specific flows' UX.

## 6. Canny

[Canny](https://canny.io/) replaces Pendo Feedback's portal directly: a public or private board where customers submit and vote, with Autopilot capturing requests from support and call tools, revenue tags weighting by MRR/ARR, and voters auto-notified on status changes. Public pricing from a free tier.

It's the feedback module alone, no analytics, no guides, which is the point if that module is all you used.

**Where it fits:** teams that want the customer-visible request queue without the suite. See our [board tools comparison](https://modem.dev/guides/canny-vs-productboard-vs-featurebase).

## Where Pendo still wins

For a product org standardizing on one platform where analytics, in-app guides, and a feedback portal share one user model, requests filterable by segment and checked against usage for those same users, Pendo's bundling is the feature, not the bug. None of the alternatives above covers all three jobs; replacing Pendo entirely means composing two or three of them. If your team is mid-market-plus, product-led, and actually uses the guides module, the un-bundling math often doesn't pay.

## How to choose

Name the module you actually use. Analytics: PostHog (self-serve, engineering-culture) or Fullstory (friction-detection-first). Research: Sprig. UX diagnosis: Contentsquare. The feedback portal: Canny. And if the feedback you care about never enters the app at all, it's in Slack threads, support tickets, and calls, that's [Modem](https://modem.dev/pendo-alternative), which also [reaches into PostHog](https://modem.dev/integrations/posthog) so the "said vs did" pairing survives the un-bundling. Our [analytics-pairing guide](https://modem.dev/guides/best-tools-to-pair-product-analytics-with-customer-feedback) covers that composition in detail.

## FAQ

### What's the best Pendo alternative for engineering teams?

The engineering-led stack is PostHog for analytics plus [Modem](https://modem.dev/pendo-alternative) for feedback: no app instrumentation for the feedback side, conversational sources covered, and topics landing in [Linear or Jira](https://modem.dev/integrations/linear) where engineers work, with the two connected so usage data and customer quotes answer together.

### Do any alternatives replace Pendo's in-app guides?

Not on this list, guides/onboarding is its own category (and often the module that keeps teams on Pendo). Fullstory and Contentsquare include lighter in-experience guides and prompts, but a dedicated onboarding tool is the real like-for-like there.

### Is there a free Pendo alternative?

PostHog's free tier is substantial on the analytics side; Canny and Modem both publish free tiers on the feedback side. Composing free tiers of an analytics tool and a feedback tool is a realistic starting stack for a small team.
