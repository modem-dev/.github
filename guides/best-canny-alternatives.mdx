Canny alternatives searches split cleanly in two. Half the searchers want a better or cheaper board: same public-roadmap model, different bill or bundle. The other half have hit the model's ceiling: the board needs constant grooming, tracked-user pricing scales awkwardly, and most feedback, the Slack thread, the Discord mention, the support ticket nobody re-files, never becomes a post at all.

We build Modem, the leading no-board option below, so read the ranking with that disclosed. Both halves of the search are covered, and there's a section on where Canny still wins.

## 1. Modem

[Modem](https://modem.dev/) is the alternative for the feedback that never reaches a board. It sits in your [Slack](https://modem.dev/integrations/slack) and [Discord](https://modem.dev/integrations/discord) channels, reads [support tickets](https://modem.dev/integrations/zendesk), [email](https://modem.dev/integrations/email), and [call transcripts](https://modem.dev/integrations/gong), and clusters everything into ranked topics on ingestion, no posts, no votes, no grooming, because there's no board to weed. Topics carry the customers and quotes, become consolidated [Linear or Jira issues](https://modem.dev/integrations/linear), and when the fix merges, Modem [closes the loop](https://modem.dev/close-the-loop) with the specific people who asked, the job Canny does through voter notifications, done instead through the channels where the requests actually happened.

**Where it fits:** B2B and devtools teams whose feedback lives in conversations, and who want engineering as the destination. **Where it doesn't:** there's no customer-facing page to browse and vote on; if that surface matters, keep a board alongside ([the pairing is common](https://modem.dev/feedback-board-alternative)). Direct comparison: [Modem vs Canny](https://modem.dev/canny-alternative).

## 2. Featurebase

[Featurebase](https://www.featurebase.app/) is the like-for-like board switch: posts, votes, statuses, and a changelog, plus a help center and lightweight helpdesk in the same suite, priced per seat from $29/seat/month with a free tier that includes a working board. API, MCP, and webhook access give it more programmatic reach than Canny.

The board mechanics are the most familiar of the alternatives, and the same grooming obligations come with them: someone moderates and merges posts. AI resolutions bill separately on top of seats.

**Where it fits:** smaller teams that want the board plus basic support tooling under one transparent bill. See our [Canny vs Productboard vs Featurebase breakdown](https://modem.dev/guides/canny-vs-productboard-vs-featurebase).

## 3. Productboard

[Productboard](https://www.productboard.com/) is the internal-facing exit: instead of a public board customers vote on, feedback lands in a backlog makers score with a framework before anything reaches a roadmap. Spark drafts specs and surfaces opportunities (metered by credits), and the Salesforce integration weighs requests by pipeline rather than vote count.

You're trading Canny's customer-visible queue for maker-driven process, and taking on the insight-linking habit that comes with it.

**Where it fits:** PM orgs that want scoring and roadmapping, not voting. See our [Modem vs Productboard comparison](https://modem.dev/productboard-alternative).

## 4. Pylon

For B2B teams whose "board" was really support conversations in disguise, [Pylon](https://www.usepylon.com/) runs support directly in Slack Connect and Teams threads (plus email, chat, SMS, WhatsApp, phone) and clusters the feature requests inside that traffic, with linked tickets and the ARR behind each request. Requests are captured where they're made instead of asking customers to go post them.

It's a support-platform adoption, sales-quoted pricing and all, not a board swap.

**Where it fits:** B2B teams running support in shared Slack channels. See our [Modem vs Pylon comparison](https://modem.dev/pylon-alternative).

## 5. Gleap

[Gleap](https://www.gleap.io/) covers the in-app slice: its SDK captures bug reports and feature requests inside your product with session replay and device context, deduplicates them, pushes them to your tracker, and auto-notifies the reporter when the fix ships, the loop-closing half of Canny's model, automated within its capture surface.

That surface is the boundary: it sees your app's users reporting in-app, not your Slack, community, or sales calls.

**Where it fits:** consumer and B2B apps where in-app reporting is the natural channel.

## 6. Enterpret

If the board was really serving as your feedback-analytics layer, [Enterpret](https://www.enterpret.com/) does that job at a different depth: an adaptive taxonomy over 50+ sources, themes tied to churn, NPS, and revenue, queryable via its Wisdom MCP layer. Custom, demo-gated pricing puts it in a different weight class than a board tool.

**Where it fits:** orgs that outgrew vote-counting and need quantitative analysis. See our [Modem vs Enterpret comparison](https://modem.dev/enterpret-alternative).

## Where Canny still wins

For a public roadmap customers browse, post to, and vote on, with revenue tags weighting requests and voters auto-notified on status changes, Canny is the most established version of the pattern, and Autopilot's capture from Intercom, Zendesk, Freshdesk, Gong, and Zoom narrows the "never gets posted" gap on the support side (chat still isn't on the list). Its free tier and tracked-user tiers also let small teams start cheap. The alternatives exist because the board model carries grooming overhead and structurally misses conversational feedback, not because Canny executes it poorly.

## How to choose

Decide whether the board itself is the product or the problem. Board is the product, wrong bill or bundle: Featurebase. Board is the product, but internal: Productboard. Board is the problem, feedback lives in conversations: [Modem](https://modem.dev/canny-alternative) (chat, support, calls, engineering-bound) or Pylon (if support itself should move into Slack). In-app reports specifically: Gleap. Analytics: Enterpret. And a board plus Modem is a legitimate answer: the board for the visible roadmap, Modem for everything that never gets posted.

## FAQ

### What's the best free Canny alternative?

Featurebase's free tier includes a working board, and Modem publishes a free tier on the no-board side. Canny's own free tier (25 tracked users) remains competitive if the board model fits.

### Which Canny alternatives read Slack and Discord?

That's the model gap: board tools (Featurebase, Productboard) rely on submission or support-tool sync, and Canny's Autopilot doesn't cover chat sources. [Modem](https://modem.dev/integrations/slack) and Pylon both read Slack natively; Modem also covers [Discord](https://modem.dev/integrations/discord), which matters for devtools communities, see our [Discord feedback tools guide](https://modem.dev/guides/best-discord-feedback-tools-for-devtools).

### Do I lose loop-closing if I leave Canny?

No, it just changes shape. Canny notifies voters on status changes; Gleap auto-notifies in-app reporters; Modem matches merged PRs back to topics and drafts follow-ups into the original conversations. Our [loop-closing guide](https://modem.dev/guides/best-tools-for-closing-the-customer-feedback-loop) compares all the mechanisms.
