Searches for Enterpret alternatives usually start from one of three places: the pricing (custom, demo-gated, no public number), the output (dashboards and taxonomies your team has to read), or the audience (it's built for CX and product analysts, and your engineers are the ones who need the signal). Which of the three is yours decides which alternative fits, so that's how this guide is organized.

We build Modem, one of the five, and Enterpret is a direct competitor of ours. The ranking reflects that; the facts in each entry are the public, verifiable ones, and we've kept a section on where Enterpret is still the right call.

## 1. Modem

[Modem](https://modem.dev/) covers the same wedge, feedback scattered across [Slack](https://modem.dev/integrations/slack), [Discord](https://modem.dev/integrations/discord), [support](https://modem.dev/integrations/zendesk), [email](https://modem.dev/integrations/email), and [sales calls](https://modem.dev/integrations/gong), with a different destination: instead of dashboards for analysts, it produces ranked topics that become consolidated [Linear or Jira issues](https://modem.dev/integrations/linear) with the customer quotes attached, or tasks [routed to coding agents](https://modem.dev/guides/best-tools-route-feedback-to-coding-agents). Any MCP-capable agent queries the same graph through the [Modem MCP server](https://modem.dev/docs/api/modem-mcp-server), available on every paid tier.

The pricing contrast is the other half of why teams evaluate the two: flat usage tiers with unlimited seats from $80/month plus a free tier, public on the [pricing page](https://modem.dev/pricing), no demo required to see a number.

**Where it fits:** the analysis should end in a filed, evidence-backed ticket, and you want to start without a sales cycle. **Where it doesn't:** Modem doesn't do churn/NPS correlation dashboards or cohort analytics; if that's the job, Enterpret still does it better. See the direct [Modem vs Enterpret comparison](https://modem.dev/enterpret-alternative).

## 2. Unwrap

[Unwrap](https://www.unwrap.ai/) is the closest like-for-like: an analytics platform clustering feedback by meaning with zero-shot NLP (no taxonomy setup), with real-time alerts, SupportIQ for support performance, and VOC reporting. Where Enterpret leans on revenue correlation, Unwrap leans on automatic theme detection, patterns register without anyone defining them first.

Pricing is also custom, from $24,000/year sized to feedback volume, with no per-seat charges, so it's an alternative on approach rather than on accessibility.

**Where it fits:** mid-market and up, standardizing feedback and support analytics under one annual contract. See our [Enterpret vs Unwrap vs BuildBetter breakdown](https://modem.dev/guides/enterpret-vs-unwrap-vs-buildbetter).

## 3. BuildBetter

[BuildBetter](https://www.buildbetter.ai/) is the accessible-pricing alternative: published credit-based tiers from $7.99/month to $1,440/month, self-serve, no demo gate. It's call-first, pulling recordings from Zoom, Gong, Chorus, Fireflies and similar, plus tickets and threads, and its output is documents: signal reports, briefs, PRDs, with Linear and Jira ticket creation from extracted signals and MCP access on higher tiers.

The tradeoff against Enterpret is breadth and analytics depth: fewer quantitative sources, and documents rather than cohort dashboards.

**Where it fits:** teams whose feedback is mostly calls and who want to evaluate without a sales conversation. See our [Modem vs BuildBetter comparison](https://modem.dev/buildbetter-alternative).

## 4. Dovetail

[Dovetail](https://dovetail.com/) comes at the same evidence problem from the research side: a customer-intelligence application where feedback and research live in one repository, and its Channels product turns feedback into evidence-backed, revenue-ranked opportunities, tracks them from surfaced to resolved, sends work into planning tools, and syncs status back from Linear and Jira. MCP, API, and CLI access exist alongside the application.

It's strongest where the team's discipline is research: consolidating interviews, tickets, and feedback into defensible findings, rather than running a continuous CX analytics operation.

**Where it fits:** research-led product orgs that want the evidence library and the opportunity tracking in one place.

## 5. Canny

[Canny](https://canny.io/) is the lightweight exit: if what you actually used Enterpret for was "which requests matter," a board with votes, revenue tags (MRR/ARR synced from Stripe, HubSpot, or Salesforce), and Autopilot's automated capture from Intercom, Zendesk, Freshdesk, Gong, and Zoom may cover it at a fraction of the weight, with public pricing from a free tier up.

The model is different: requests live as board posts, which means grooming, and conversational feedback that never becomes a post stays invisible.

**Where it fits:** teams that were over-tooled on analytics and mostly need a prioritized, customer-visible request list. See our [board tools comparison](https://modem.dev/guides/canny-vs-productboard-vs-featurebase).

## Where Enterpret still wins

Honesty section: if your org needs feedback themes quantitatively tied to churn, NPS, CSAT, and expansion across 50+ sources, with an adaptive taxonomy maintained at enterprise scale, none of the five above replicates that depth. Enterpret's Wisdom MCP layer also brings that analysis into Slack, Claude, and Cursor as a query surface. The alternatives exist because that depth comes with a sales-led motion and an analyst-shaped output, not because the analytics are weak.

## How to choose

Name the thing that sent you looking. Demo-gated pricing: BuildBetter and [Modem](https://modem.dev/pricing) publish numbers, Canny publishes tiers. Dashboard output your team doesn't read: Modem files tickets, BuildBetter writes documents, Canny keeps a board. Analyst audience when your engineers need the signal: that's the [Modem](https://modem.dev/enterpret-alternative) case specifically. Same analytics, different vendor: Unwrap. Research culture: Dovetail.

## FAQ

### What's the main difference between Modem and Enterpret?

Destination and motion. Enterpret produces analytics for people who read dashboards, with ticket creation as a configured path; Modem produces ranked topics whose default next step is a consolidated tracker ticket or a coding-agent task, self-serve from $80/month. Full comparison: [Modem vs Enterpret](https://modem.dev/enterpret-alternative).

### Is there a free Enterpret alternative?

Modem and Canny both publish free tiers. BuildBetter's entry tier is $7.99/month. Enterpret and Unwrap are both custom-priced with no public free tier.

### Which alternative works with AI agents over MCP?

Modem (every paid tier, OAuth), BuildBetter (higher tiers), and Dovetail all expose MCP access. Unwrap doesn't publicly document one. Our [MCP servers for customer feedback guide](https://modem.dev/guides/best-mcp-servers-for-customer-feedback) compares the options.
